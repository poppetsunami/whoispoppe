# Focused Homepage Repositioning Design

## Objective

Broaden the portfolio’s first-read story from primarily UX/design/research to an accurate combination of product strategy, research leadership and enablement, experience and service design, operational systems, and hands-on AI product experimentation. Preserve the six-case-study limit and avoid presenting Poppe as having held titles she has not held.

## Positioning

The primary identity is **Product & Experience Strategist · Research Leader**.

The hero through-line is:

> I work inside complex systems to understand what’s really happening, define the right problems, and turn research into product strategy, better workflows, and useful technology.

The supporting credibility statement is:

> 12+ years across enterprise platforms, operational systems, connected products, regulated environments, and emerging technology.

## Homepage Information Architecture

The homepage contains seven conceptual sections:

1. Hero
2. What I Do
3. AI & Emerging Technology
4. Selected Work
5. Systems I’ve Worked In
6. How I Work
7. Experience plus About, contact, and footer

The existing Strategic Impact module remains as compact supporting evidence near the top. It must not create a separate conceptual narrative or repeat positioning copy.

## What I Do

Create three evidence-led pillars:

1. **Product Strategy** — discovery and problem framing, product vision, MVP definition, roadmap prioritization, product requirements, experiment design, adoption strategy, and stakeholder alignment.
2. **Research Leadership & Enablement** — research-practice development, mentoring, continuous discovery, repositories and knowledge reuse, taxonomy and ontology, research tooling, governance, and organizational influence.
3. **Experience & Service Design** — operational research, workflow mapping, service design, systems thinking, prototyping, interaction design, and physical-digital experiences.

## AI & Emerging Technology

Create a compact four-card evidence section:

1. **Research Intelligence & AI Enablement** — synthesis capacity, retrieval, institutional knowledge, and evidence reuse.
2. **Human + AI Experience Strategy** — expert-led and AI-assisted workflows, trust, provenance, bias evaluation, human review, and accountable decisions.
3. **AI Product Experimentation** — MVP definition, conversation intelligence, sentiment analysis, agentic workflows, MCP/connectors, and governed knowledge experiences.
4. **Building with AI** — direct prototyping with Claude and Codex, reusable skills and agents, tool/data connections, and implementation-led learning.

The copy must distinguish tools Poppe builds with from confidential client or product names.

## Selected Work

Keep exactly six cards. Preserve the existing titles and anonymization rules.

- **Agricultural Equipment Service Platform** receives the descriptor “Product discovery, service strategy, and workflow transformation for agricultural equipment service.” Its first-read evidence surfaces the three-month discovery, dealer research, roadmap prioritization, two Product Owners, legacy constraints, operational redesign, and dual-track delivery.
- **Kroger Warehouse Claims Platform** receives the descriptor “Modernizing an enterprise warehouse claims system through operational research and service design.” Its evidence explicitly connects warehouse operations, workflow mapping, systemic pain points, business rules, product requirements, and the redesigned system.
- The remaining cards receive only light copy edits needed to strengthen product strategy, AI, or operational-system evidence.

Only Kroger and KAMP may remain named. Public rendered source must not contain Lilly, Culligan, John Deere, SmartConnect, or MOUX.

## Systems I’ve Worked In

Add a concise visual band with five domains:

- Healthcare & Clinical Operations
- Agriculture & Machinery Service
- Warehouse & Supply Chain
- Enterprise & Connected Platforms
- AI & Knowledge Systems

Each domain includes a short description and references the type of portfolio evidence associated with it. This is not a second project grid.

## Consolidation

- Keep Discover, Frame, Strategize, Make, and Align in How I Work, while shortening repeated explanations.
- Remove the AI-native callout from How I Work because the dedicated AI section replaces it.
- Remove Leading Product Design as a standalone homepage section.
- Absorb its useful leadership evidence into Research Leadership & Enablement and How I Work.
- Tighten About Me so it adds personality and community leadership without repeating the AI section.
- Retain the Experience accordion.

## Metadata and Sharing References

Update all site-controlled references to match the new positioning:

- Document title and meta description
- Open Graph title, description, image alt text, and site identity
- Twitter title, description, and image alt text
- The rendered social-sharing thumbnail
- Accessible labels relevant to the changed content

The canonical URL remains `https://whoispoppe.com/`. The flower identity remains in the favicon and social thumbnail.

## Responsive and Accessibility Requirements

- Preserve the current visual system: `#F7F7F7`, `#111111`, `#666666`, and `#6EDFA3`.
- New sections use semantic headings, readable line lengths, descriptive labels, and keyboard-independent content.
- Three- and four-card layouts collapse cleanly on mobile without horizontal scrolling.
- Motion follows existing patterns and cannot be required to understand content.

- Run the complete content test suite and production build.

- After publishing, verify live HTTPS HTML metadata and the 1200×630 social image.
